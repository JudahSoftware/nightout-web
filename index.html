How it works

From your camera roll to everyone who was there

Nothing to set up first. Take pictures the way you always do.

1

Take pictures as usual

New photos and videos show up in Recent by themselves, by day. It's your inbox for the night.

2

Pick tonight's

Tick the good ones and tap New Grouping. It names itself, like Sat night · Hayes Valley · 5, and stays private.

3

Send to who was there

Pick people, a group, or type a phone number. They get it in their Feed, and you see who has looked.

The app

Four tabs. That's all of it.

Feed

Everything sent to you, everything you sent, and your private groupings. Newest first.

Recent

What you just took. Send it, group it, add it to My Media, or let it go.

Media

Every picture, video and recording you've kept, by month and day. Your favorites have a star.

People

Your people and your groups, like "Sea Ranch Crew". Send to a whole group in one tap.

Friends without the app

They don't need the app to see it

Send to a phone number and they get a text with a link. A 6-digit code opens everything you shared with them, right in their browser.

  • No account and no download for them
  • They can comment, and you see it in the app
  • Nothing is public: every picture needs a sign-in or a fresh code

Everything else

Built for the morning after

Private

Private until you send it

Nothing you take is seen by anyone until you choose who gets it. A marker shows what you've shared.

Take back

Change your mind

Take back a send and it leaves everyone's Feed. Remove a picture from a grouping any time.

Seen

See who has looked

Each person shows "Seen Sun 11:02 PM" or "Not seen yet". Resend to the ones who haven't.

Together

Likes, replies, comments

React under each picture and each night. Everyone who was there can add what they took.

Edit

Crop and captions

Trim a video to the good part or crop a picture by its corners. Add a caption everyone sees.

Audio

Record the moment

Pictures, videos and audio all live together. Record straight into a night out.
